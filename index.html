About

Hi! I am a 3rd year PhD candidate in Computer Science at the University of South Carolina. I am part of the AI Institute and the CSCE Department, working with Dr. Vignesh Narayanan at the Dynamical Systems and AI Research group since Fall 2024. In Summer 2026, I was at Argonne National Laboratory, where I worked on developing a distributed reinforcement learning framework to address non-stationarities in multi-agent systems under partial observability. Before that, I completed my Master's in Data Science from Chennai Mathematical Institute, India in 2023 and my Bachelor's in Statistics from the University of Calcutta in 2021. My research broadly focuses on AI for collaborative autonomy, with an emphasis on developing adaptive, safe, and resource-efficient learning and decision-making methods. I am particularly interested in the theoretical foundations of learning-based systems, with a focus on convergence, stability, safety, and efficiency.
